About Ginhawá

The story, the craft, and the people behind every bag.

Our Story

Carried with intention, made to last

Ginhawá began with a simple frustration: bags that looked beautiful for a season and fell apart the next. We started working with small, family-run tanneries and hardware makers who cared as much about longevity as they did about looks — and built a brand around bags meant to be used, not just carried.

Every piece is cut, stitched, and finished by hand in small batches, so quality control happens at every step rather than at the end of a line. "Ginhawá" means ease and comfort — the feeling we want you to have every time you pick up one of our bags, whether it's for a morning commute or a weekend away.

What We Stand For

A few principles that guide every bag we make.

Craftsmanship

Full-grain leather, hand-set hardware, and reinforced stitching at every stress point — details you feel for years, not just on day one.

Responsible Sourcing

We work with tanneries and mills that meet independent environmental standards, and we design every bag to be repaired, not replaced.

Community

A portion of every sale supports the artisan workshops we partner with, and we publish where and how each collection is made.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what we hear most. Still stuck? Contact us.

How long does shipping take?

Domestic orders arrive in 3–5 business days after processing. International orders typically take 7–14 business days, depending on destination and customs processing. You'll receive a tracking link as soon as your order ships.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original condition and packaging. See our full Return & Refund Policy for details on how to start a return.

How do I know which size or style to choose?

Each product page lists dimensions and recommended uses, and our Compare Bags page breaks down capacity, materials, and best use across every bag type we carry.

What materials do you use?

Most styles are crafted from full-grain or pebbled leather with brass or matte hardware; some casual styles use waxed canvas or suede. Exact materials are listed in each product's description.

How should I care for my bag?

Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth and condition every few months. Keep suede away from water and brush regularly. Full care instructions for each bag type are on our Compare Bags page.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and cash on delivery in select areas, all selectable at checkout.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to most countries. Rates and delivery estimates are calculated at checkout based on your address. See Shipping Information for more.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email. You can also view your order status any time from Order History in your account.

Shipping Information

What to expect from checkout to your doorstep.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. During peak seasons or sales events, processing may take slightly longer — we'll always email you if there's a delay.

Domestic Shipping

Standard domestic shipping is a flat $12, and free on orders over $150. Most domestic orders arrive within 3–5 business days of shipping.

International Shipping

We ship to most countries worldwide. International rates and delivery windows (typically 7–14 business days) are calculated at checkout based on your address and may be subject to customs fees or import duties charged by your local authority.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. Logged-in customers can also view live status from Order History.

Delays & Issues

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated window, reach out via our Contact Us page with your order number and we'll look into it right away.

Return & Refund Policy

Simple, fair, and hassle-free.

Return Window

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange, provided they're unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with tags attached.

How to Start a Return

Log in and visit Order History to find your order, or contact us via our Contact Us page with your order number. We'll send you a prepaid return label and next steps within one business day.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. You'll receive an email confirmation once your refund has been processed.

Exchanges

Need a different color or size? Note it when you start your return and we'll prioritize shipping the replacement as soon as the original item is received.

Non-Returnable Items

Final-sale and clearance items, gift cards, and any bag showing signs of use beyond inspection are not eligible for return or refund.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your bag arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos and your order number, and we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund.
